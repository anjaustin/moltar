# Maintainers

This document outlines the maintenance, governance, and stewardship of the moltar repository.

## Repository Stewardship

### Mission
Moltar exists to provide a rigorous, auditable platform for security research with a focus on:
- **Methodological Integrity**: Research conducted to the highest scientific standards
- **Open Science**: Transparent methods, data, and results sharing
- **Educational Value**: Resources for learning proper research methodology
- **Security Advancement**: Contributions to the field of security engineering

### Core Values
- **Scientific Rigor**: All research follows established scientific methodology
- **Transparency**: Open processes and decision-making
- **Quality**: High standards for code, research, and documentation
- **Ethics**: Responsible research conduct and impact assessment
- **Accessibility**: Lowering barriers for newcomers while maintaining standards

## Decision-Making Process

### Minor Changes
- **Code fixes**: Direct merge after review
- **Documentation updates**: Direct merge after review
- **Minor features**: PR review and approval

### Major Changes
- **New research methodologies**: Peer review required
- **Breaking changes**: Community consultation
- **Security-related changes**: Independent security review
- **Repository structure changes**: Thorough discussion and consensus

### Research Decisions
- **Experimental protocols**: Pre-registration and peer review
- **Publication decisions**: Independent validation required
- **Ethical concerns**: Ethics review board consultation

## Escalation Process

### Issue Resolution
1. **Initial Response**: Acknowledge within 24 hours
2. **Investigation**: Assess issue within 3-5 business days
3. **Resolution Plan**: Develop solution approach
4. **Implementation**: Execute fix or workaround
5. **Follow-up**: Confirm resolution and document

### Conflict Resolution
1. **Direct Discussion**: Attempt resolution through dialogue
2. **Mediation**: Involve neutral third party if needed
3. **Escalation**: Repository owner final decision if required
4. **Documentation**: Record resolution for future reference
